# Gates County, NC — GIS County Card

## Summary

Gates County (markets **[Eastern NC]**, FIPS **37073**, slug **gates**, `cntyfips='073'`) publishes public AGOL FeatureServers under **services3.arcgis.com/nJbIFHiSnaX0z0hS** (AGD / Gates Parcel App). **Wire-first parcels:** **GatesParcelService/FeatureServer/5** — ~**8,014** polys with owner (`name1`/`name2`), mailing (`address1`/`city`/`state`/`zipcode`), situs (`formattedpropertyaddress`), `acres`/`legallandunits`, full tax (`assessedlandvalue`/`totalbuildingvalue`/`marketvalue`/`deferredvalue`), **on-layer sale** (`saleprice`/`deeddate`/`bookpage`), zoning attr (`zoning`), and embedded **PRC** AppraisalCard URL. ~**2,331** with `acres` 5–150 (~**877** with saleprice in band; ~**1,345** vacant/no bldg in band). **Cities-first:** only incorporated municipality is **Gatesville** (Towns FS/9; ~**283** parcels intersecting town polygon) — **no separate city zoning FS**; county **Zoning FS/6** (~**8,027**) is countywide (incl. town). **FLU:** DEQ-certified County Comp Plan (2017) + Town of Gatesville Plan (2025) PDFs — **no FLU FeatureServer** (Land_Cover_Poly is vegetation, not FLU). **PA:** ITSPublicGT `AppraisalCard.aspx?id={pclnbr}` (PDF) + NCPTS `parcel-detail/{pclnbr}` + AGD print `?pclnbr=` + jurisdiction Parcel Viewer. - **OneMap ID swap** — `parno`=pin, `altparno`=pclnbr (opposite of many NC counties)
- **AGD shapefile download** `storage.googleapis.com/dl.agdmaps.com/downloads/gates/gatesshp.zip` → **404** as of verify
