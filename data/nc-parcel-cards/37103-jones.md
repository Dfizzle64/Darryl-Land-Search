# Jones County, NC — GIS County Card

## Summary

Jones County (markets **[Eastern NC]**, FIPS **37103**, slug **jones**, OneMap `cntyfips='103'`) publishes public AGOL FeatureServer **Jones_Bitek** under AGD org `services3.arcgis.com/nJbIFHiSnaX0z0hS` (owner `tpenegar`). **PRIMARY parcels:** **Jones_Bitek/FeatureServer/0** — ~**9,526** polys with owner (`name1`/`name2`), mailing, situs `formattedpropertyaddress`, `calc_acre`/`acres`/`deed_acres`/`legallandunits`, full tax (`marketvalue`/`assessedvalue`/`assessedlandvalue`/`totalbuildingvalue`/`deferredvalue`), **on-layer sale** (`saleprice`/`deeddate`/`deedbook`/`deedpage`), and embedded **PRC** AppraisalCard + **Print_** AGD print URLs. ~**2,351** with `calc_acre` 5–150 (~**854** saleprice in band; ~**1,500** vacant/no bldg in band). **Cities-first:** **Trenton** (seat), **Pollocksville**, **Maysville** via municipal FS/21 (~276 / ~265 / ~734 parcel intersects) — **no municipal or county zoning FeatureServer**; town zoning = ordinance + Official Zoning Map at Town Hall (PDF ordinances for Maysville + Pollocksville; **Trenton zoning online gap**). Unincorporated county = **unzoned** (2013–2033 LUP explicitly separates zoning). **FLU:** Jones County Land Use Plan 2013–2033 PDF (Existing + Future Land Use maps) — **no FLU REST**. **PA:** ITSPublicJN2.0 `AppraisalCard.aspx?id={pin83|parcelnumber}` (PDF) + NCPTS `parcel-detail/{pin83}` + AGD print `?pin83=` + jurisdiction Web App / Experience Builder. Markets: **[Eastern NC]**.
