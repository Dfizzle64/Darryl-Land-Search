# Pitt County, NC — GIS County Card

## Summary

Pitt County (Greenville MSA, FIPS **37147**, slug **pitt**, OneMap `cntyfips='147'`) publishes rich public cadastral + planning REST at `gis.pittcountync.gov`. **PRIMARY parcels:** **PittOpenData/CadastralPitt** MapServer/0 — ~**81,371** polygons with owner, mailing, situs (`PhysicalAddress`), calculated/deeded acres, last sale (`SalesPrice`/`SalesMonthYear` + `StampSalePrice`), full tax split (`CurTaxValue`/`CurLandValue`/`CurBuildValue`/`CurXFeatValue`), and parcel `Zoning` attr. ~**7,919** with `CalculatedAcres` 5–150 (~**5,705** `CurBuildValue` 0/null; ~**3,760** `SalesPrice`>0). **Cities-first zoning** via county `Permitting/PermitDisplayMap` town layers + **City of Greenville** OpenData/gucdatashare (`ZONE`). Unincorporated covered by **ZoningPitt**. **FLU:** Greenville FutureLandUse + Horizons 2026; county FutureLandUse2045 (study-area partial) + ExistingLandUse. **PA:** NCPTS `{PARCELNUMBER}` / `{REID}` + OPIS. Markets: **[Greenville]**.
