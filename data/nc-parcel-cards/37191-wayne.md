# Wayne County, NC — GIS County Card

## Summary

Wayne County (Raleigh–Durham / eastern Triangle footprint, FIPS **37191**, slug **wayne**) publishes rich public cadastral + zoning REST via **WayneCountyNC-GIS** AGOL (`services5.arcgis.com/q2nSlChj7QgGTANO`) and **City of Goldsboro** (`gis.goldsboronc.gov`). **PRIMARY parcels:** AGOL **TaxParcels / Property Lines** FS/14 — ~**70,919** polygons with owner (Name1/2), mailing parts, situs `PropertyAddress`, GIS + legal acreage, land/bldg/market/assessed values, last sale (SalePrice/SaleYear/SaleMonth + Deed*), `REID`, `PropUse`, VacantImproved. ~**9,822** with `GIS_Acres` 5–150 (~**6,982** building value 0/null; ~**3,866** SalePrice>0). **Cities-first zoning:** Goldsboro (city REST + AGOL mirror), Mount Olive, Fremont, Eureka, Pikeville on county AGOL; unincorporated **Wayne County Zoning** (~1,198). **FLU:** Goldsboro **Proposed_Land_Use** only (~2,989) — no countywide FLU REST. **NC OneMap** (`cntyfips='191'`) fallback (~70.7k; situs on polys). **PA:** Tyler ITSPublic + NCPTS. Markets: **[Raleigh-Durham]**.

## Gaps

- **No countywide FLU** — Goldsboro Proposed_Land_Use only
- **No zoning REST** for Seven Springs or Walnut Creek
- **No dedicated city GIS hosts** for Mount Olive / Fremont / Pikeville / Eureka — county AGOL publishes their zoning
- **NCPTS parcel-detail** is SPA-routed (`parcelId` internal); TaxBillSearch/Parcel/{PIN} is the durable GET deep-link verified with pre-populate
- **ITSNet** Basic Search is POST/session-oriented for full cards
- **OneMap vs county GIS_Acres** 5–150 band differs (~15.2k vs ~9.8k) — prefer county LegalLandUnits/GIS_Acres
- **Goldsboro Planning/Addresses** incomplete vs county Address Points
- **PARCEL2** token-gated; **MI Wayne** AGOL parcel shape must not be wired
- **EnerGov_Zoning** stub — do not prefer
- Last-sale only; no multi-transfer sales history REST
- MaxRecordCount 2000 (AGOL) / 4000 (Goldsboro Parcels) — paginate
- No phones/emails; no AADT/utilities; no paid vendors
