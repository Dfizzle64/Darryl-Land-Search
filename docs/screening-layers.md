# Site screening layers

Public map layers for a first look at flood, wetlands, schools, and utilities. They do not change the acreage band or the existing parcel filters. Every toggle starts **off**.

Nothing here is a will-serve letter, a survey, a jurisdictional determination, or an insurance quote. If a market has no public layer, the drawer says **unknown**. It does not invent a provider, a grade, or a phone number.

## What you can turn on

| Layer | Where it draws | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flood zones | Raster overlay, from about zoom 8 | [FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer](https://hazards.fema.gov/arcgis/rest/services/public/NFHL/MapServer), layer 28 (flood hazard zones). Map export tiles. |
| Wetlands | Raster overlay, from about zoom 11 | [USFWS National Wetlands Inventory](https://fwspublicservices.wim.usgs.gov/wetlandsmapservice/rest/services/Wetlands/MapServer). This is the national layer, so it covers Florida and the other states in the app. The service itself stops drawing past about 1:100,000. |
| Schools | Dots in the current view, plus Orange County, Mecklenburg, Cobb, and DeKalb attendance-zone tiles | Florida 2025-26 letter grades from the public [Know Your Schools](https://edudata.fldoe.org/ReportCards/Schools.html) report card. The School Grades Excel workbook on fldoe.org returns 403 from many hosts, so this app does not re-download it and does not use a secondary grade list. Orange County zones are live: elementary layer 91, high school layer 90, middle school [InfoMap layer 66](https://ocgis4.ocfl.net/arcgis/rest/services/InfoMap_Public_Layers/MapServer/66). Mecklenburg zones are live from county GIS: [elementary](https://meckgis.mecklenburgcountync.gov/server/rest/services/CMSElementarySchoolDistricts/MapServer), [middle](https://meckgis.mecklenburgcountync.gov/server/rest/services/CMSMiddleSchoolDistricts/MapServer), and [high](https://meckgis.mecklenburgcountync.gov/server/rest/services/CMSHighSchoolDistricts/MapServer), with campus points from [CMSPublicSchool](https://meckgis.mecklenburgcountync.gov/server/rest/services/CMSPublicSchool/FeatureServer/0). Charlotte-Mecklenburg letters are the official [2025-26 SPG file](https://accrpt.tops.ncsu.edu/docs/spgdisag_datasets/SPG_Disag_2025-26.zip) for `lea_code` 600, subgroup ALL. Cobb County School District zones are the public [ccsdschoolzonemapwm](https://gis.cobbcounty.gov/gisserver/rest/services/cobbpublic/ccsdschoolzonemapwm/MapServer) layers 0, 1, and 2. DeKalb County School District zones are the public [elementary](https://services3.arcgis.com/3TzhpgpIaE4cOUGc/arcgis/rest/services/Elementary_School_Attendance_Areas/FeatureServer/0), [middle](https://services3.arcgis.com/3TzhpgpIaE4cOUGc/arcgis/rest/services/Middle_School_Areas/FeatureServer/0), and [high](https://services3.arcgis.com/3TzhpgpIaE4cOUGc/arcgis/rest/services/High_School_Attendance_Areas/FeatureServer/0) FeatureServers (there is no MapServer export). Forty Cobb parcels and forty DeKalb parcels also show GOSA 2025 CCRPI single scores for the zoned school. Georgia does not publish A–F letters. Other North Carolina dots stay on the 2024-25 [DPI researcher file](https://www.dpi.nc.gov/data-reports/school-report-cards/school-report-card-resources-researchers). Other states plot [NCES locations](https://services1.arcgis.com/Ua5sjt3LWTPigjyD/arcgis/rest/services/Public_School_Locations_Current/FeatureServer/0) and link the state report card. Gray means no letter in this extract. |
| Water | Orange County polygons only | [Orange County open data](https://ocgis4.ocfl.net/arcgis/rest/services/AGOL_Open_Data/MapServer), layer 60 (`SERVEDBY`). Charlotte Water has no public service-area polygon. Inside the City of Charlotte the drawer names that jurisdiction as a proxy and does not draw a polygon. Unincorporated Mecklenburg stays unverified. |
| Sewer | Orange County polygons only | Same Orange County service, layer 61. Charlotte sewer uses the same jurisdiction proxy as water. |
| Electric | Orange County polygons; HIFLD outside that county | Orange County layer 68 (`COMPANY`) inside the county. Outside it, [HIFLD electric retail service territories](https://services3.arcgis.com/OYP7N6mAJJCyH6hd/ArcGIS/rest/services/Electric_Retail_Service_Territories_HIFLD/FeatureServer/0). In Mecklenburg that layer returns Duke Energy Carolinas and EnergyUnited EMC, and it can also return a municipal retailer on the same point. Neither is a connection or a will-serve. |
| Gas | No overlay | No public gas service-area layer, including Mecklenburg County, Cobb County, and DeKalb County. The drawer says unknown. |

Selecting a parcel or a tract looks up flood, utilities, and attendance zones at that point (tracts use the Census internal point) and lists nearby public schools within 3 miles. Forty Cobb County parcels (`data/fixtures/screening/cobb-batch40.json`, county FIPS 13067) and forty DeKalb County parcels (`data/fixtures/screening/dekalb-batch40.json`, county FIPS 13089) use the researched school, flood, and utility fields instead of that live lookup. Opportunity Zone designation, tract income, and AADT are not part of those joins. In Mecklenburg County the drawer also reports the 2020 census tract `geoid20` from [county GIS](https://meckgis.mecklenburgcountync.gov/server/rest/services/2020CensusTracts/FeatureServer/0). That id does not change Opportunity Zone designation, and it is not a second tract overlay. Flood reads `STATIC_BFE`. The value `-9999` means no published static base flood elevation, and the drawer does not turn that sentinel into a number. The community id is NFHL political layer 22. The FEMA Community Information System page for North Carolina is not used — it returned 403 from the research host — and this app does not invent a Community Rating System class. Wetlands use a roughly 70-foot box because the NWI service does not answer a bare point.

## Contact

The parcel drawer shows the **owner mailing address already on the parcel extract**. If that address is empty, it says so. It does not look up a phone or an email.

- County property appraiser: Orange County still deep-links the parcel id. Other cataloged Florida counties open that county’s appraiser search. A county that is not cataloged is left as a gap — it is not sent to the Orange County appraiser.
- Entity owners (LLC, Inc, and similar tokens in the assessor name): Florida opens Sunbiz with the name filled in. Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Alabama open that state’s secretary-of-state search. Georgia, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Alabama do not accept a prefilled name, and the link says so.

## Gaps

- Water and sewer outside Orange County, Florida. Charlotte Water publishes no service-area polygon. The City of Charlotte boundary is a jurisdiction proxy only. Unincorporated Mecklenburg, and the other Mecklenburg towns, stay unverified. Hillsborough County’s utilities folder is geocoding tools, not service areas.
- Gas. Orange County’s open-data map has water, sewer, and electric service areas, and no gas polygon. Mecklenburg County, Cobb County, and DeKalb County have no public gas polygon either, including the 80 joined parcels.
- Cobb water and sewer polygons are not drawn county-wide. The drawer names CCWS or a city service boundary for the 40 joined parcels. Outside a city polygon, CCWS is a documented jurisdiction, not a connection. Five of those parcels are in a Sewer Not Anticipated polygon and stay unknown. The other Cobb parcels stay on the same unknown water and sewer wording as every county outside Orange and Mecklenburg’s city proxy.
- DeKalb water and sewer polygons are not drawn. The drawer names DeKalb DWM, or Atlanta DWM for two City of Atlanta parcels, as a documented jurisdiction. That is not a connection. The other DeKalb parcels stay on the same unknown water and sewer wording as every county outside Orange, Mecklenburg’s city proxy, and this batch.
- Electric outside Orange County is still the HIFLD retail territory, which can overlap (a downtown Orlando HIFLD query returns more than one utility; northern Mecklenburg can return Duke Energy Carolinas, EnergyUnited EMC, and a town retailer together). Inside Orange County the drawer uses layer 68 instead, which is one service-area company. On the DeKalb batch, an overlap of Georgia Power with Snapping Shoals EMC or Walton EMC keeps the cooperative.
- School letter grades are Florida 2025-26, Charlotte-Mecklenburg 2025-26 (LEA 600 from `SPG_Disag_2025-26`), and other North Carolina schools 2024-25. The DPI school-report-card researcher zip is still the 2024-25 file; the 2025-26 letters used here are the accountability SPG zip, subgroup ALL. Florida letters come from the Know Your Schools report card. Confidence stays medium until the School Grades Excel workbook can be downloaded — that file returns 403 from many hosts. Four CMS schools are marked alternative-model in that file and are shown without an A–F letter. Georgia, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Alabama dots link the state report card and do not show a made-up grade. The Cobb and DeKalb batches are the exception for a score, not a letter: those 80 parcels show the GOSA 2025 CCRPI single score for the zoned elementary, middle, and high school. Georgia stopped publishing A–F letters after 2022-23. Two DeKalb parcels are inside the City of Atlanta and still zone to Druid Hills DCSD.
- An empty FEMA response is **unknown**, not Zone X. An empty NWI hit is “no polygon at this centroid,” not a permit answer.
- There is no “hide floodway” filter. Turning that on would query FEMA for every parcel.

## Refresh

School grades are a server fixture, not a live call on every map move. Locations for non-Florida schools are queried live from NCES.

```bash
npm run seed:schools
```

That reads the Florida report-card API, the NCES CCD directory, and the North Carolina `rcd_acc_spg1.xlsx` file, then writes `data/fixtures/screening/school-ratings.json`. The Florida workbook on fldoe.org blocks a lot of scripted downloads; the script uses the same public report-card API the Know Your Schools page uses. Re-run it when a new grade year is published and update the year strings in `scripts/seed_school_ratings.py` and `src/lib/screening.ts`.

Charlotte-Mecklenburg 2025-26 letters are a separate file, `data/fixtures/screening/cms-spg-2025-26.json`, taken from `SPG_Disag_2025-26.zip` (`lea_code` 600, subgroup ALL) and joined to NCES ids with the CCD directory. `npm run seed:schools` does not refresh that file. Replace it when NCDPI posts the next SPG zip.

Cobb batch-40 is `data/fixtures/screening/cobb-batch40.json`. Rebuild it from the research parcel JSON with `python3 scripts/build_cobb_batch40_fixture.py <dir-of-13067-json>`. The flood mix in that file is 28 Zone X, 11 Zone AE, and 1 Zone A. A static BFE is stored only where NFHL published one (three parcels, 861 ft NAVD88). Gas is unknown on all 40. Five sewer rows stay gaps because the county layer says sewer is not anticipated.

DeKalb batch-40 is `data/fixtures/screening/dekalb-batch40.json`. Rebuild it from the research parcel JSON with `python3 scripts/build_dekalb_batch40_fixture.py <dir-of-13089-json>`. The flood mix in that file is 31 Zone X (one of them the 0.2% annual-chance subtype) and 9 Zone AE (five of them floodway). No static BFE was published on these 40 centroids, so none is stored. Gas is unknown on all 40. Water and sewer are a jurisdiction proxy on all 40: DeKalb DWM for 38, Atlanta DWM for the 2 City of Atlanta parcels.

Flood, wetlands, utilities, NCES locations, Cobb attendance-zone tiles, and DeKalb attendance zones are live ArcGIS queries (`/api/screening/point`, `/api/screening/schools`, `/api/screening/utilities`, `/api/screening/dcsd-zones`). No key. Vector layers wait until the view is about a county wide so a statewide polygon dump never lands in the browser.
